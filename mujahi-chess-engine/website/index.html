Core Architecture

Designed for predictable execution on constrained 512 MB containers with strict process recovery.

Single-Search Multi-Depth

Request [5, 10, 15] in a single call. Stockfish executes one iterative deepening run to depth 15 and captures or streams snapshots at depths 5, 10, and 15 without repeating earlier plies.

Session-Isolated Scheduler

Client session IDs prioritize latest-position-first execution, cancel obsolete requests from the same client only, and deduplicate identical FEN requests across concurrent users.

Semantic FEN & UCI Safety

Every FEN is validated for board rank sums, king count, non-adjacent kings, opposing-king check legality, castling rook/king squares, and en passant geometry before touching Stockfish stdin.

Render Hosting Tiers

Free tier is supported for testing and cold verification; Starter or higher is recommended for active play.

Render Tier Resources Recommended Config Expected Behaviour
Free 0.1 vCPU · 512 MB RAM THREADS=1 · HASH=64 Sleeps after 15 min idle (15–50s wake). Suitable for testing and verification only; no consistent production SLA.
Starter 0.5 vCPU · 512 MB RAM THREADS=1 · HASH=64–96 Always-on container, zero sleep cold starts, reliable low-depth extension responsiveness.
Standard 1.0+ vCPU · 2 GB RAM THREADS=1–2 · HASH=128–256 Higher NPS throughput for deeper 15–18+ ply lines and multi-user queues.
